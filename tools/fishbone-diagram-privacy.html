Ishikawa Fishbone Diagram - Privacy

// SOLO: NOTHING SENT / ONLINE BOARDS: PLANNED

The fishbone diagram works in two modes. Solo mode, the only one available today, runs entirely in your browser. Online boards are planned and not built; this page says now what they will store, so the terms are known before anyone signs in. When online boards open, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" stamp will change.

This page describes how the tool is built and how it will be operated. It is not a contract, a certification, or a compliance statement. It is written by the one person who runs the site and has not been audited by anyone else. No claim is made of GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA or any other regulatory compliance, so do not put anything on a diagram that would matter under one of them. Provided as-is, without warranty, and used at your own risk. The site-wide notice carries the full terms.

1. What is sent and stored

  • Solo mode: nothing is sent to any server. Projects are kept in this browser's localStorage; view settings and whether you have seen the guide are kept there too.
  • Online boards (planned): a board's contents (problem statement, categories, causes, statuses, evidence, owners, locks), the name the site owner gave each member, and the time of each edit. The site owner's access list will hold each person's email address, name, and a keyed hash of their access phrase.

2. What is not stored

  • The access phrase itself: only its hash, made with a server secret.
  • Email addresses on boards: a board stores a random member id and a name, never an email. The email list is used only to sign in and is never shown to other members.
  • IP addresses, except as a hash salted daily, used only for rate limits.
  • Analytics identifiers or application cookies.

3. Where data is sent

  • Solo mode: nowhere. Exports, project files and snapshot links are made in your browser.
  • A snapshot link carries the diagram inside the part of the link after #, which browsers do not send to the server that hosts this page. Whoever you send the link to can read it.
  • Online boards (planned): fishbone.jesse-anderson.net, a Cloudflare Worker with Durable Object storage. Cloudflare Turnstile will load from challenges.cloudflare.com only when someone clicks "Go online".
  • Web fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and request headers when the page loads. No diagram content is sent to Google.

4. How long it is kept

  • Solo mode: until you delete the project or clear this site's data.
  • Online boards (planned): one year after the last edit, extendable by a champion to three years, with a banner 30 days before deletion. A champion can delete a board at any time.

5. Who can see it

  • Solo mode: anyone with access to this browser, and anyone you send a file, an export or a link to.
  • Online boards (planned): every member of a board can read everything on it, including its history. Brainstorm ideas will show no author, and votes will show totals only.
  • The site owner will see board metadata (title, champions, member names, size, last edit, expiry) through scripts, not board contents. No script, endpoint or page will read a board's contents for the owner. The operator could read storage technically and does not.

6. Your controls

  • Delete a project, or clear this site's data, to remove solo projects from this browser.
  • Online boards (planned): a champion can delete a board. Ask the site owner to remove you from the access list; with erasure, your name is replaced with "Former member" on every board and in every history entry.

7. Platform processing and backups

Online boards (planned) will run on Cloudflare's network. Whether Durable Object storage keeps a point-in-time recovery window, and how long, has not been confirmed. It will be stated here before online boards open.

8. Security

  • Online traffic will use HTTPS. Nothing will be end-to-end encrypted.
  • The access phrase is seven random words generated for you. It is not a password: never reuse it elsewhere, and never type one of your own passwords in its place. The page will refuse to send anything that looks like a password.
  • Board ids will travel in the part of the link after #. A board link alone lets nobody in; access is membership.

9. Children

This tool is not directed at children and collects no age information.

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes will update the "Last updated" stamp at the foot of this page and, where it matters, show a notice in the tool.

11. Contact
